# Denta+ V4 visual system

Owner authority: DentaPlus_V4_Master_Redesign_Codex_Prompt.md. This replaces rejected V3 as the implementation direction; it is not a claim of Owner acceptance.

The clinic task comes first: an agenda-led desk, search-driven patient directory, chart-first patient workspace, contextual booking inspector and canonical financial ledger. Platform operators and kiosk patients use independent compositions and authority boundaries. Statistics support work rather than forming a repetitive KPI wall.

Forest #162421 anchors navigation, welcome and operator context. Pine #0F6B63 marks primary action. Canvas #E2DACB, toolbar #EAE3D5 and reading surfaces #F3EFE6 retain the warm reference identity; copper #C85A32 is a restrained accent. Semantic warning/success colors require a real state and visible text. No invented clinical safety, cash or appointment status.

Inter / Noto Sans Arabic. Meaningful body text generally 14-16px, entities 16-20px and page headings 29-34px. Use logical direction properties, tabular money and explicit bilingual labels. Desktop grouped navigation is 216px; mobile uses contextual navigation and real records rather than clipped tables.

One foundational `v4-system.css`; workspace styles own composition. `v4-icons.css` owns first-party outline geometry with retained functional aliases. Existing DataTables, Select2, FullCalendar, form selectors and authorized routes remain operational contracts. Shared modal focus, inert background and opt-in unsaved-value confirmation are presentation behavior, not new business policy.

Use opaque warm reading surfaces, modest offset elevation and 7-12px controls/panels. Avoid repeated decorative gradients, blur, color-coded module cards and cascades of compatibility overrides. Native clinical SVG, drawing/rendering logic and legal/print evidence remain protected.

Completion is recorded per route/state in docs/UI_V4_DELIVERY.md and the private local route ledger. A render or screenshot alone does not establish functional or visual acceptance.
